# DataRelay Atlas

**Map your engineering. Connect your AI.**

DataRelay Atlas connects engineering methodology, project/lifecycle state, durable knowledge, and AI context across software projects.

It is designed so humans and AI agents can work from the same current, attributable engineering context without replaying old conversations or treating a Wiki as a competing source of truth.

## What Atlas connects

- **Methodology** — Data Relay Labs Engineering System
- **Projects** — repositories, products, architecture, specifications, ADRs
- **Lifecycle** — development, validation, review, release, operations
- **Knowledge** — searchable project and cross-project engineering context
- **AI context** — scoped context for Cursor, ChatGPT, and MCP-capable agents

## What Atlas does not replace

Atlas does not aim to replace GitHub, CI/CD, issue trackers, coding agents, or general-purpose collaboration tools. It observes and connects engineering evidence from those systems.

## Current phase

Atlas is in initial product-definition and architecture bootstrap. The first implementation phases focus on project registration, canonical source synchronization, provenance, retrieval, and AI/MCP access.
